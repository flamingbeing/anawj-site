# Study-day runbook (prewarm app)

One page for the team. Address: **prewarmstudy.anawj.com** (install it to the home screen). Questions: the study lead.

## Before the case
1. Open the app with signal. Tap **Update available** at the top if it shows (do this between patients, never mid-case).
2. Top bar chip says **Synced** (green). If it says *N waiting*, *N not saved* (red) or *Offline*, tap it: it refreshes and
   says why. *No hospital*: Settings, pick your hospital.
3. Your initials are set (Settings). On your team (Settings shows the team name).
4. **PACU nurse link:** make a fresh link for this patient on the PACU page, open it once on a phone that is **not** on the
   team (or a private window) and tap something; it should say "Saved to the team". Then send it to the PACU nurse.
5. New patient: Subject ID, group. Check the hospital shows on the patient line.

## During the case
- Readings: tap the box, type three digits (366 = 36.6), the pad moves to the next box, Save. Grey numbers are only
  defaults carried over: change them or leave them blank.
- Alarms only buzz while the app is open and in front. Keep the phone unlocked with the app on screen. A missed buzz
  repeats every 2 minutes for 10 minutes.
- Don't tap Update available during the case.

## After the case
1. Mark each section **complete** (pre-warm, intra-op temps, BP/HR/BIS, intra-op details, PACU), then **Data collection
   complete**.
2. Wait for the chip to say **Synced** before leaving the signal area.
3. **Do not use Log out** to "tidy up": it deletes every patient from the phone. If anything is *waiting* or *not saved*,
   it warns you; cancel and get signal first.
4. POD1 and POD2 show on the patient list as due; fill them from the chart and tick complete.

## When sync fails
- Red chip "N not saved": tap it for the reason. *Refused (locked)*: ask a study admin to unlock. *No hospital*: set it in Settings.
- "N waiting": the phone has no signal; readings are kept and sent when it returns. Keep the app installed and don't clear
  browser data.
- Stuck on an old version (e.g. Firefox): open **anawj.com/prewarm-update/** and tap Get the latest version.
- Nurse sees "This link has stopped working": make a new link on the PACU page and send it again.
- Last resort: Export (Settings) gives an Excel file from this phone.
