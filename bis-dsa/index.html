From the BIS sensor to the DSA

The BIS number is one summary of a frontal EEG. The density spectral array (DSA) shows the whole spectrum over time, so you can see why the number is what it is. Move the drug and depth controls and watch the raw EEG, the number and the DSA change together.

The screen is laid out like a BIS monitor: the number with its SQI (signal quality) and EMG bars, then the BIS and EMG trends, the DSA, and the raw EEG. The keys at the bottom show the DSA or the EEG large. On the DSA, time runs left to right, frequency goes up (0–30 Hz) and colour shows power; the white line is SEF95, the frequency below which 95% of the power sits, and the purple line is the median frequency (MF, 50%). Messages from the monitor (artefact, isoelectric EEG, out of target range) appear in the status line. The real monitor measures EMG at 70–110 Hz; this simulator stands in a muscle term, because it samples at only 128 per second.

How one DSA column is made

Every second the monitor takes the newest 2 seconds of EEG and turns it into one thin coloured column. This animation uses the live EEG from the monitor above, so change the drug and the next column changes too.

    When the number misleads

    • Ketamine and nitrous oxideKetamine adds fast (gamma) power, so BIS often reads 60–80 in an unconscious patient. Nitrous oxide barely moves BIS. Look at the DSA and the clinical picture.Source: Purdon 2015, Anesthesiology; Akeju 2016.
    • Muscle (EMG) and paralysisFrontalis activity sits in the high frequencies and pushes BIS up. Check the EMG bar. If the alpha band is unchanged, treat the cause: analgesia first, relaxant only if surgery needs it (paralysis hides movement, which may be the only sign of lightening). Under relaxant, BIS fell to 44–47 in awake volunteers (Schuller 2015). The flip side: paralysis can lower BIS in a patient who is awake, so a low number under relaxant doesn’t rule out awareness.Source: Schuller 2015, BJA; Messner 2003.
    • Older brainsAlpha power is about 9 dB lower at 70–90 than at 18–38, and slower (Purdon 2015). Indices may read higher at a dose that already causes unconsciousness. Older patients need less drug; watch SR and the raw EEG before deepening.Source: Purdon 2015, BJA.
    • Read SR, not just BISSuppression often appears with BIS 35–45, especially in older patients; SR above 0 means suppression whatever the number (Bruhn 2000).Source: Bruhn 2000, J Clin Monit Comput.
    • LagBIS is smoothed (10, 15 or 30 s), and the whole delay before it settles at a new level ranged from about 15 s to over 2 minutes in testing (Pilge 2006). The DSA and raw EEG change first.Source: Pilge 2006, Anesthesiology.
    • Low BIS that isn't drugHypothermia, hypoglycaemia, hypotension with poor cerebral perfusion and cerebral ischaemia all slow or flatten the EEG. So can paralysis in an awake patient, pulse artefact from a sensor over the temporal artery, blinks or head movement that look like slow waves, paradoxical delta during arousal, and pre-existing brain disease or a low-voltage EEG. A sudden fall with no dose change: check glucose, blood pressure and perfusion before lightening.Source: Mathew 2001; Schuller 2015; García 2021.
    • InterferenceForced-air warmers, oscillating ventilators and pacing or ECG spikes add high-frequency noise that can push BIS up (warmer off: true value back in 2–3 min); diathermy swamps the signal. Check the raw EEG, EMG bar and SQI.Source: Hemmerling & Fortier 2002; BIS Complete operator’s manual.
    • Validated in adultsBIS ranges come from adults. In infants under about 4 months there is no frontal alpha under sevoflurane, and BIS is unreliable; interpret cautiously in neurological disease and with psychoactive drugs.Source: Cornelissen 2015, eLife.

    What the trials say

    Processed EEG is a tool, not a target. The main evidence in brief:

    StudyFinding
    B-Aware 2004In high-risk patients, BIS-guided anaesthesia cut awareness (2 vs 11 cases) compared with routine care.
    B-Unaware 2008 / BAG-RECALL 2011BIS was not better than an end-tidal anaesthetic gas alarm protocol at preventing awareness.
    NAP5 2014Accidental awareness about 1:19,600 overall, about 1:8,200 with a muscle relaxant; most at induction or emergence.
    ENGAGES 2019EEG-guided anaesthesia in older patients did not reduce postoperative delirium.
    Balanced 2021 (delirium sub-study)Targeting BIS 50 rather than 35 gave delirium 19% vs 28%. A secondary outcome: 1-year mortality, the primary outcome, was no different (Short 2019, Lancet).
    AAGBI/SIVA TIVA guideline 2018Use processed EEG when TIVA is given with a muscle relaxant.
    NICE DG6Depth-of-anaesthesia monitors are an option in patients at higher risk of awareness or excessively deep anaesthesia, and in all patients having TIVA.
    A teaching simulation. The EEG is synthetic and the BIS value comes from a simple model of spectral features (edge frequency, fast and slow power, suppression ratio) tuned to typical published ranges. It is not the proprietary BIS algorithm and must not guide patient care.
