### Professionalism

1. Is conscientious, reliable, punctual, takes personal responsibility for lapses and seeks help to resolve complex interpersonal situations*
2. Exhibits professional and ethical behaviour towards :
   - Fellow healthcare workers: is respectful and looks out for other team members
   - Patients and their families: maintains patient confidentiality*

### Interpersonal & Communication Skills

3. Patient- and Family-Centered Communication  
   Communicates effectively and sensitively with patients and their families; effectively manages conflict*
4. Develops/Maintains accurate medical records and communicates care of patients appropriately within the healthcare system*
5. Uses close loop communication to verify understanding; maintains effective communication during crisis situation; manages feedback effectively for self and others*

### Systems-Based Practice

6. Patient Safety and Quality Improvement  
   Ensures patient safety and is committed to improving quality of service*
7. System Navigation for patient-centered care  
   Coordinates care effectively for patients with other health care professionals and performs safe and effective transitions of care within and across healthcare delivery systems*

### Practice-Based Learning

8. Evidence-based and Informed Practice
   - Keeps up-to-date with knowledge and skills
   - Uses evidence based medicine to guide individualized patient care*
9. Reflective Practice and Commitment to Personal Growth  
   Is reflective, accepts and asks for feedback, and self-directed in seeking improvement*
