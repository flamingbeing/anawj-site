Privacy

APMES Logbook (apmes.anawj.com/logbook) and APMES Evaluations (apmes.anawj.com/evals) · last updated 13 October 2026

In short

  • These are independent apps for anaesthesia residents. They are not an official NUH or NUHS service.
  • Your logbook is yours. Other residents, faculty and programme staff see only counts, never your cases or reflections (details below).
  • No advertising, no tracking or analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Enter de-identified information only: patient initials and a short clinical description. No names, NRIC/FIN, hospital numbers or identifiable photos.

What is stored

  • Your account: the name and email of the Google account you sign in with (Firebase Authentication).
  • Logbook: the cases you log (date, patient initials, details, categories, tags), your reflections and their figures, your portfolio details (Section 1), templates and settings.
  • Evaluations: the requests you send or receive (the item, a de-identified case card, the assessor) and the completed assessment forms.
  • Feedback you send from the logbook (More → Send feedback): your text, the name of the page you were on, the app version and browser type, linked to your account ID (not your email).
  • Programme lists: resident and faculty names and emails kept by the programme admins and directors.

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase project in Singapore, asia-southeast1). Your phone or computer also keeps an offline copy so the app works without signal; signing out removes it. Some display settings (such as the theme) are kept on your device only.

An assessor sees the evaluations sent to them. These permissions are enforced by the database's security rules, which are published with the app's source code. People who administer the Google Cloud project can technically access stored data; they do so only to run and fix the service.

Optional connections you choose to use

  • Save to Google Drive: asks for permission to create files in your Drive (drive.file). The app can see only the files it created, in an "APMES Logbook" folder. The access token stays in your browser.
  • Save to Dropbox: the app gets its own folder (Apps/APMES Logbook) and nothing else in your Dropbox. The access token stays in your browser.
  • Connect Claude (advanced): a connector you run on your own computer that lets an AI assistant read your own logbook. What it reads is sent to that assistant's provider. Check your hospital's policy before using it.

You can remove these at any time: in your Google Account (Security → third-party connections), in Dropbox (Settings → Connected apps), or by signing out of the connector.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like any website, these see your IP address and browser details when pages load. Sign-in and data go to Google (Firebase).

Keeping and deleting data

  • You can delete your own cases, reflections and figures at any time. Deleted items stay in your recycle bin for 30 days, then are removed.
  • To delete your whole account and logbook, or to get a copy of everything, use the app's export and backup, or ask the app owner (below).
  • Evaluations are part of your training record and are kept for the programme. Ask a programme director if something needs correcting.

Contact

Questions, corrections or deletion requests: ask your programme's logbook admin, or open an issue at github.com/flamingbeing/anawj-site (that page is public, so don't include personal or patient details there).
