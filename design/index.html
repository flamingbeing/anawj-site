NUHS style guide

Colours, type and components taken from nuhs.edu.sg, so the NUH tools on this site look at home next to it. Everything below is drawn with tokens.css.

Unofficial. Read from the public site's stylesheet in October 2026. It copies the look, not the identity: don't use the NUHS logo or name to make a tool look official.

Character

What the site does
  • Navy and white. Navy carries the heavy parts (side panel, footer, primary buttons). Most of the page is white.
  • Light, large headings. Headings are Open Sans 300 in navy. Weight comes from size, not boldness.
  • Colour as a thin line. Orange, red and cyan show up as stripes and underlines, rarely as fills.
  • Square, flat blocks. Bands go edge to edge with no rounding and no shadow. Only buttons get a 5px radius.
  • Two-up feature bands. Half photo, half soft colour with a centred light heading and an outline button. Sides alternate.
Where to improve on it
  • White text on cyan (the site's active tab) is 2.7:1. Use navy text on cyan instead.
  • White on periwinkle and on the bright blue band is below 4.5:1. Keep text there at 24px+ or use navy.
  • Don't use cyan for text on white. Use --n-blue for links.
  • Keep a visible focus ring: 3px navy on light grounds, cyan on navy. Cyan on white is only 2.7:1, under the 3:1 a focus ring needs. The site mostly relies on browser defaults.

Type

Open Sans (Google Fonts), weights 300, 400, 600 and 700. Base 16px, line height 1.5. h1 to h3 are Light in navy; h5 and h6 are SemiBold in navy. Section titles on coloured bands use SemiBold link blue.

Buttons and inputs

5px radius, 6px × 12px padding, regular weight. The outline button ("View More") is the most common one on the site: it takes the text colour of whatever band it sits on.
